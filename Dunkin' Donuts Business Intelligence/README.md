## 📖 Overview

This project explores how Dunkin' Donuts can leverage Business Intelligence (BI) to transform operations, improve customer experience, and gain a competitive edge. By implementing dashboards, predictive models, and advanced analytics, Dunkin' can become a more data-driven organization.

## 📊 Key Objectives

- Integrate data from point-of-sale systems, loyalty programs, and social media.
- Provide dashboards for sales, customer segmentation, and sentiment analysis.
- Enable data-driven decision-making for marketing, product development, and operations.
- Address ethical challenges and ensure responsible innovation.

---

## ✨ Proposed BI Solutions

1. **Customized Donuts on the Go**
   - Self-serve stations with dynamic toppings.
   - BI insights on top-selling combinations and upsell recommendations.

2. **Healthier Menu Expansion**
   - Introduce protein boxes, oatmeal bowls, yogurt parfaits.
   - Use BI to analyze customer preferences and track sales performance.

3. **Sales Performance Dashboard**
   - Interactive maps, sales comparisons, and target tracking.
   - Identify top-selling products and marketing campaign impacts.

4. **Customer Segmentation Dashboard**
   - Segment customers using RFM analysis and demographics.
   - Track campaign performance and integrate customer feedback.

5. **Social Media Sentiment Analysis**
   - Analyze online brand perception.
   - Track sentiment trends and inform marketing strategies.

6. **KMeans Segmentation Analysis**
   - Cluster customers by demographics and behavior.
   - Enhance marketing precision and customer insights.

## ⚙️ Ethical Considerations

- Reduce food waste from customization.
- Ensure transparency in healthy menu claims.
- Prioritize worker safety and fair compensation.
- Maintain product quality at value price points.

## 📍 Business Impact
