## 📖 Overview

This project focuses on predicting stock market trends using advanced machine learning models. By analyzing historical stock price data, we aim to develop predictive models that forecast future stock prices and market trends, supporting better investment decisions and strategy development.

## 💡 Business Proposition

The project was developed for an AI-based hedge fund startup to:
- Predict stock market trends with high accuracy and reliability
- Provide investors with informed and proactive trading strategies
- Enhance portfolio management and risk mitigation

---

## 📊 Dataset & Sources

- **Sources:** UCI Machine Learning Repository, Kaggle
- **Data:** Daily price info for various stocks and ETFs listed on NASDAQ
  - Open, high, low, close prices
  - Trading volume
  - Technical indicators (RSI, EMA, volume spikes)

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Visualized price and volume trends over time
- Analyzed technical indicators across different market phases (bullish, bearish, sideways)
- Conducted correlation analysis to identify key predictors for stock price movements

---

## ⚙️ Models & Techniques

- **ARIMA:** Time series forecasting for non-seasonal patterns
- **Prophet:** Robust time series forecasting with seasonality and holiday effects
- **XGBoost:** Gradient boosting model for predicting trend direction
- **Cross-validation:** Model tuning and performance optimization
- **Performance metrics:** MSE, RMSE, accuracy

---

## ✨ Key Achievements

- Built automated data pipelines for historical stock data
- Trained ARIMA, Prophet, and XGBoost models, achieving a 15% improvement in stock trend prediction accuracy over baseline.
- Achieved strong performance in forecasting trends and price movements
- Developed actionable insights to guide investment decisions
